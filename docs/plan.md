## Conventions

- Chapter numbers always two digits (`02`, `03`, …) matching the book.
- Slugs are lowercase, hyphenated, short (`machine-learning`, `deep-learning`, `nlp`).
- A chapter is `done` only when: examples transcribed, exercises attempted, and the user has written something in `notes.md`.
